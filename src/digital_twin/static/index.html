    04AI systems

    The retrieval is running on this page.

    Answers here are not generated from a prompt and hoped for. A hybrid retriever scores every chunk of the CV corpus two ways: Okapi BM25 for term weighting, character-trigram cosine for the morphological variants BM25 misses. Reciprocal rank fusion then merges the two rankings by position, so an unbounded BM25 score cannot swamp a bounded cosine. Type anything and watch all three stages.
